# Step 2 — Solve It With Plain Python

**You already know everything needed for this step.**
No new library. No new import. Nothing new at all.

Read each English instruction.
Translate it into Python yourself.
Then type it.

---

## Rule for this step

> **Read the sentence. Say it out loud. Then write the code.**

If you cannot say it in English, you cannot write it in Python.

---

## Part A — Store the data

### English
> Remember the app name we are studying.

### Your translation
A single piece of text stored in a labelled box.

---

### English
> Remember the minutes spent on Instagram for each of the last 7 days.

### Your translation
Many numbers, in order, in one container.

**Use these 7 values so the whole class can compare:**

```
95, 120, 80, 140, 60, 170, 110
```

---

## Part B — Total usage

### English
> Start a counter at zero.
> Go through every value one at a time.
> Add each value to the counter.
> Show the final counter.

### Your translation
Three ingredients: a starting variable, a loop, an addition.

**Predict first:** What total do you expect?

---

## Part C — Average usage

### English
> Take the total.
> Share it equally across the number of days.
> Show the result.

### Your translation
One division. But be careful *which kind* of division.

**Predict first:** Will the answer be a whole number or a decimal?

---

## Part D — Highest usage day

### English
> Assume the first day is the highest so far.
> Go through every value.
> If a value is bigger than the current highest, that value becomes the new highest.
> Show the highest.

### Your translation
A starting guess, a loop, a comparison, a replacement.

**Think:** Why do we start with the *first value* and not with zero?

---

## Part E — Lowest usage day

### English
> Same idea as highest.
> But this time keep the smaller one.

---

## Part F — Days above average

### English
> Start a counter at zero.
> Go through every value.
> If the value is greater than the average, increase the counter by one.
> Show the counter.

### Your translation
A counter, a loop, a condition, an increment.

**Predict first:** Out of 7 days, how many do you think will be above average?

---

## Part G — Show your report

### English
> Print a small report showing:
> the app name, total minutes, average minutes,
> highest day, lowest day, and days above average.
