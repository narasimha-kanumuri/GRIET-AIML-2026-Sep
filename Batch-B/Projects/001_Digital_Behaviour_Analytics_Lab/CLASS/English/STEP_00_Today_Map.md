---

## The question for today

> **You think you use your phone for about an hour a day.**
> **Is that true?**

You are not going to argue about it.
You are going to **measure** it.

---

## What you will build

A small system that takes your daily app-usage data and answers:

1. How much time went to each app?
2. Which day was your heaviest day?
3. Which days were above your own average?
4. How much did you actually study?
5. Is your digital life balanced?
6. Can a single picture show all of this?

---

## The KEY for today

> ## **Data = Digital Mirror**

A mirror does not argue with you.
It does not flatter you.
It just shows what is there.

That is what data does.

---

## How today will run

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Generate your own dataset |
| 2 | Solve the problem with plain Python you already know |
| 3 | Break it, then fix it |
| 4 | Discover why plain Python gets painful |
| 5 | Meet NumPy — the fast maths engine |
| 6 | Meet Pandas — Excel controlled by Python |
| 7 | Draw the answer |
| 8 | Challenge + what surprised you |

---

## Class rules for today

1. **Predict before you run.** Always guess the output first.
2. **Type the code.** Do not copy it from anywhere.
3. **Errors are not failure.** They are the lesson.
4. **If you are stuck for 2 minutes, raise your hand.**

---

## What you will be able to say at the end of today

> "I took raw data, calculated it with NumPy, organised it with Pandas,
> drew a chart, and made a decision from it."

That sentence is a Data Analyst job description.
